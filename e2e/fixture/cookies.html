Cookie Policy

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our services.

Categories we use

We group cookies into four categories. Strictly necessary cookies are required for the website to function and cannot be switched off. Functional cookies remember choices such as your language. Analytics cookies help us understand how the site is used. Advertising cookies are used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant adverts on other sites.

Third parties

Some third-party cookies are set by partners we work with, including analytics providers and advertising networks. We do not control those cookies.

How long they last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for up to 24 months from your last visit, depending on their purpose.

Your choices

You can manage or withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the preferences link in the footer. You may also change your browser settings to refuse cookies, although parts of the site may then stop working.
